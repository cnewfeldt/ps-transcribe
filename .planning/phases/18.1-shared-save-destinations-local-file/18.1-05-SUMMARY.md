## Decisions Made

- **Always stream meetings/memos to localFileRoot/<subfolder> regardless of localFileEnabled** (RESEARCH Open Question §1). The toggle gates whether the Local File is the destination of record vs. a transient working file; preserves the streaming TranscriptLogger semantics that are critical for crash recovery (STAB-01).
- **Inline SaveDestinations.save in stopSession** instead of extracting a helper. ContentView.stopSession is already long; adding a private helper would obscure rather than clarify the post-session sequence. The transient toggle pattern is short enough to read in place.
